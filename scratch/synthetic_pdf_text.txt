Proportionate Deduction Rule
When a member voluntarily selects a room category above the eligible category, room charges are not payable
above the eligible limit. For surgeon, nursing, procedure, and other charges that are contractually linked to
room category, the admissible amount may be reduced in the same proportion as the eligible room cost bears
to the actual room cost. The application should cite this section when explaining a room-category What-If
scenario.
Example for application testing only: If the eligible single private room is INR 8000/day and the selected room is INR
12000/day, the room cost is not fully admissible and linked proportionate deductions may reduce the insurer share. This
example is illustrative and not an additional contractual limit.

What-If Calculation Principle
●
Preserve the same uploaded policy and policy_id across baseline and changed scenarios.
●
Change only the requested variable unless the user explicitly changes multiple variables.
●
Recalculate insurer and patient share deterministically from the same policy rules.
●
Explain material changes using the policy evidence that caused the delta, such as the non-network co-pay or
room-category rule.
●
Never substitute a demo policy for the active uploaded policy.
