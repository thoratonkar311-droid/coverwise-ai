\# CoverWise AI



\## Policy-to-Patient: Insurance Coverage \& Treatment Cost Intelligence



CoverWise AI analyzes insurance policy documents and helps users understand:



\- What a treatment may be covered for

\- Deductibles and co-payments

\- Estimated patient responsibility

\- Estimated insurer responsibility

\- Policy clauses supporting the calculation

\- Important assumptions and warnings



\## Architecture



Frontend

→ FastAPI Backend

→ AI/RAG Pipeline

→ Deterministic Rules Engine

→ PostgreSQL

→ Results Dashboard
